II — an apology

I’m sorry,
pyari rabbit.

I know I’ve been missing. Not from your life, but from your days — the calls, the texts, the small “did you eat?” moments that used to come so easily.

I’m not hiding behind “busy”. Busy is never a good enough reason for you to feel alone. I’m genuinely, truly sorry.
